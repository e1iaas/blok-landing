Screen Time not working on your child's iPhone? 9 fixes

Updated 8 November 2026

When Screen Time stops working on a child's iPhone, there are two usual causes: the settings haven't synced from your phone to your child's, or your child has found a loophole. Update iOS on both phones, restart them, and set a Screen Time passcode your child doesn't know. Then close the loopholes below one by one.

When the settings don't reach your child's iPhone

  1. Update iOS on both phones. Apple has fixed several Screen Time bugs in updates, so check Settings → General → Software Update on your phone and your child's.
  2. Restart both phones. It sounds too simple, but it often makes stuck settings sync.
  3. Check that your child is in your Family Sharing group with a child account, and that both phones are signed in to iCloud. Screen Time for a family member syncs through iCloud.
  4. Turn Screen Time off and on again for your child from your phone, then set the limits again. Write them down first.

When your child gets around the limits

  1. Set a Screen Time passcode that's different from the phone's passcode, and don't type it where your child can see it.
  2. Keep the date and time automatic. In Settings → General → Date & Time, leave Set Automatically on, so changing the clock doesn't move the limits.
  3. Stop apps from being deleted and reinstalled. Under Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases, set Deleting Apps to Don't Allow, and turn on Ask to Buy.
  4. Close the browser gap. If an app is limited but Safari isn't, many apps work in the browser too. Limit Safari as well, or limit websites under Content & Privacy Restrictions.
  5. Check Always Allowed. Every app in that list ignores Downtime. Keep it to Phone, Messages and what your child really needs.

What no setting can stop

  • A factory reset removes every parental control, on every phone.
  • A second device, like an old phone or a friend's tablet, isn't covered by the settings on your child's iPhone.
  • Messages and Phone can carry links and calls even during Downtime if you allow them, and that's usually what you want.

That's why the rules you agree on together matter as much as the settings.

Questions

Why are my Screen Time limits not working on my child's iPhone?

Usually the settings haven't synced from your phone, or your child has found a loophole. Update iOS and restart both phones, check Family Sharing and iCloud, and set a Screen Time passcode your child doesn't know.

How do kids get around Screen Time?

The most common ways are knowing the Screen Time passcode, changing the clock, deleting and reinstalling apps, and using an app's website in Safari instead. Each one can be closed in Settings.

Why does Downtime not block some apps?

Apps in the Always Allowed list keep working during Downtime. Open Screen Time for your child, tap Always Allowed and remove anything that shouldn't be there.
