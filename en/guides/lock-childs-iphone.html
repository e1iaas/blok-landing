How to lock your child's iPhone remotely

Updated 4 November 2026

You can lock the apps on your child's iPhone from your own iPhone in two ways: with Apple's built-in Screen Time through Family Sharing (Downtime and App Limits), or with a parental control app like Blok, which turns it into one LOCK button with timers and schedules. Both rely on Screen Time, so the lock holds when the phone restarts.

Option 1: Apple Screen Time (free, built in)

Screen Time can be managed from the parent's iPhone when the child is part of your Family Sharing group. On recent iOS versions:

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings → Screen Time and tap your child's name under Family.
  2. Tap Downtime to lock everything except the apps you allow, either right away or on a schedule (for example 20:00–07:00).
  3. Tap App Limits to set a daily time limit for single apps or whole categories such as Games or Social.
  4. Under Always Allowed, keep Phone, Messages and any app your child should always be able to use.
  5. Set a Screen Time passcode that your child doesn't know, so the settings can't be changed on their phone.

Screen Time is powerful but spread over several menus, and it's built around daily limits and schedules more than "phones away now".

Option 2: lock in one tap with Blok

Blok: Parental Control uses the same Screen Time technology, but gives you one button on your own iPhone.

  1. Install Blok on your iPhone and on your child's iPhone (iOS 16 or later), and choose Parent and Child.
  2. Pair the phones with the code shown on your phone, approve Screen Time on your child's phone and pick the apps to lock, for example games and social media.
  3. Tap LOCK on your phone. Lock until you unlock, for 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours, or on schedules for school hours and bedtime.

Your phone shows when your child's phone has confirmed the lock. Settings on the child's phone are protected by a parent PIN, and Blok doesn't read messages, track location or collect what your child does.

What a lock does, and doesn't do

  • Only the apps you chose are locked. Phone and Messages can stay open, and emergency calls always work.
  • The lock is enforced by iOS, so it holds if the app is closed or the phone restarts.
  • An app that's already on screen is locked the next time your child switches apps or unlocks the screen.
  • If your child's phone is offline, the lock arrives when it's back online.
  • No parental control can stop a factory reset of the phone.

Questions

Do I need an app to lock my child's iPhone?

No. Apple's Screen Time can lock apps through Downtime and App Limits when your child is in your Family Sharing group. A parental control app like Blok makes it faster: one tap to lock now, with timers and schedules.

Can my child still call me when their iPhone is locked?

Yes. You choose which apps are locked, so Phone and Messages can stay open, and emergency calls always work.

Can I lock my child's iPhone if it's turned off or offline?

The lock is sent right away and applies as soon as the phone is back online. In Blok, your phone shows when your child's phone has confirmed the lock.

Can I lock an Android phone the same way?

Screen Time and Blok are for iPhone only. Android phones use Google Family Link instead.
