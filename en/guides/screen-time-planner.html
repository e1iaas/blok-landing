Screen time planner for kids

Enter your child's age, school hours and bedtime. The planner gives you a daily screen time limit based on Sweden's Public Health Agency guidance, and a lock schedule for school and bedtime that you can print and put on the fridge.

Your family's screen time plan

  • Leisure screen time a day: at most 1–2 hoursat most 2–3 hours
  • School lock, weekdays: 08:00–15:00
  • Bedtime lock, every night: 20:30–07:00
  • Free time after school: 5.5 h, of which at most 2 h on screens

That's a lot of free time. A homework lock, for example the first hour after school, makes the limit easier to keep.

Phone and Messages can stay open during every lock, so your child can always reach you.

Put the plan on your child's iPhone

Blok: Parental Control locks the apps you choose on your child's iPhone with schedules for school and bedtime, and with one tap from your own phone for dinner and homework. No tracking, and it doesn't read messages.

Questions

Where do the screen time numbers come from?

From Folkhälsomyndigheten, Sweden's Public Health Agency, 2024: at most 1 to 2 hours of leisure screen time a day for ages 6 to 12, and 2 to 3 hours for ages 13 to 18. Schoolwork, music, podcasts and e-books don't count.

Is my data saved anywhere?

No. The planner runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent or stored.
