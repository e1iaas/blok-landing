Best parental control apps for iPhone (2026)

Updated 5 November 2026

The best parental control app for an iPhone depends on what you want it to do. To set limits for free, start with Apple's built-in Screen Time. To lock apps in one tap from your own phone, use a lock app like Blok. To read alerts about messages and content, use a monitoring app like Bark. For a family with both iPhone and Android, choose a cross-platform app like Qustodio.

First decide: lock, limit or monitor?

Parental control apps do three different jobs, and most families only need one or two of them:

  • Lock: apps stop working when you say so, for example at dinner, during homework or at bedtime.
  • Limit: a daily time budget for games or social media, and filters for adult websites.
  • Monitor: the app looks at messages, searches or location and tells you what it finds.

Monitoring tells you the most, but it also reads the most of your child's private life. Many parents of 8 to 14 year olds start with locks and limits, and add more only if there's a real reason to.

How to choose

  1. Try Screen Time first. It's free, and for many families Downtime plus a few App Limits is enough.
  2. If the daily fight is "phones away now", get a lock app. One button on your phone beats walking to your child's room and opening Settings.
  3. If you have a specific safety worry, look at monitoring. Talk to your child about it first; secret monitoring tends to break trust when it's found.
  4. If your family mixes iPhone and Android, pick a cross-platform app. Otherwise you'll manage two systems.

Questions

Is there a free parental control app for iPhone?

Yes. Apple's Screen Time is free and built into every iPhone. It can set Downtime, App Limits and content filters, and you can manage it from your own iPhone through Family Sharing.

Can kids get around parental controls on an iPhone?

Apps built on Screen Time are enforced by iOS, so closing the app or restarting the phone doesn't remove the lock. Keep your Screen Time passcode or parent PIN secret, and keep iOS updated. No parental control can stop a factory reset.

Do parental control apps read my child's messages?

Some do. Monitoring apps scan messages and content to send you alerts. Lock and limit apps like Screen Time and Blok don't read messages.

Which parental control app works on both iPhone and Android?

Cross-platform apps like Qustodio and OurPact run on both. Apple Screen Time and Blok are iPhone only, and Google Family Link manages Android phones.
