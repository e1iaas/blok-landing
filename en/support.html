The lock doesn't take effect

  • Check that your child's phone is online. The lock arrives once it's connected again.
  • An app that's already open is locked the next time your child switches apps or unlocks the screen.
  • Open Blok on your child's phone and check that you've picked the apps to lock. No apps picked means nothing is locked.
  • Screen Time must be approved on your child's phone. Open Blok there; the app tells you if anything is missing.

I forgot the parent PIN

Open Blok on your phone, go to Settings and tap your child's phone. The recovery code is there.

Changing or re-pairing a phone

On your phone: Settings, tap your child's phone and choose "New pairing code". Enter the code in Blok on your child's phone.

Subscription

  • Cancel: Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • New phone? Tap "Restore purchases" in Blok.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Unlocking always works, even without a subscription.

Delete your account and data

In Blok on your phone: Settings, then Delete account. Everything is removed. You can also email us. Read more in our privacy policy.
