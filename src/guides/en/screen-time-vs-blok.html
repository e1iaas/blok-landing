Apple Screen Time vs Blok: which do you need?

Updated 4 November 2026

Apple's Screen Time is free and built into every iPhone; it's best for daily app limits, content filters and activity reports. Blok: Parental Control builds on Screen Time and adds a one-tap remote lock from the parent's iPhone, with timers and schedules and a clear locked or unlocked status. Many families use Screen Time for filters and Blok for "phones away now".

When Screen Time is enough

If you mainly want a daily budget for games, a fixed bedtime and filters for websites and age ratings, Screen Time does all of it at no cost. Set it up once with a passcode your child doesn't know.

When Blok helps

Daily limits don't fit the moments that come up: dinner, homework, a family outing. Blok is for those. Tap LOCK on your phone and the apps you picked are locked on your child's iPhone, until you unlock or for a set time, and your phone shows when the lock has arrived. Blok reads no messages, tracks no location and has no ads; data is stored in the EU.

Questions

Is Blok a replacement for Screen Time?

No. Blok runs on Apple's Screen Time technology and adds a remote lock button, timers and schedules. You can keep using Screen Time's limits and filters alongside it.

Is Screen Time enough for young children?

Often, yes. Content filters, Downtime and App Limits cover most needs. Families add Blok when they want to lock apps on the spot from their own phone.

Does Blok track my child?

No. Blok blocks apps; it doesn't read messages, track location or collect which apps your child uses.
