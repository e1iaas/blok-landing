Questions

What should a phone contract for kids include?

Where and when the phone is used, what to do before downloading apps, safety rules for strangers and personal information, a promise to tell a parent when something feels wrong, and what happens if a rule is broken. Keep it short enough to remember.

At what age should we make a phone contract?

When your child gets their first phone, often between 9 and 12. Review it every few months, and give more freedom as your child shows they can handle it.

Is anything I type saved?

No. The template maker runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent or stored.
