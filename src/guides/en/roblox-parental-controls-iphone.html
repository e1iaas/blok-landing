Roblox parental controls on iPhone

Updated 8 November 2026

Roblox has its own parental controls: link a parent account to your child's Roblox account, and you can set a daily time limit, a spending limit for Robux, and who your child can chat with. On the iPhone itself, Ask to Buy stops surprise purchases, and Screen Time or Blok can lock Roblox completely during homework and at bedtime.

1. In Roblox: link a parent account

  1. Open Roblox on your child's iPhone and go to Settings → Parental Controls.
  2. Choose to add a parent and follow the steps. You'll need to confirm that you're an adult, for example with an ID or a face age check.
  3. From your own account, on roblox.com or in the Roblox app, set a daily screen time limit and a spending limit, and choose who can chat with your child.
  4. Set a Parental PIN, so your child can't change the settings.

Roblox changes its menus now and then; if a step looks different, check Roblox's help center for the current steps.

2. On the iPhone: purchases and time limits

  • Ask to Buy in Family Sharing means every Robux purchase needs your approval on your phone.
  • App Limits in Screen Time can give Roblox a daily time budget, for example one hour on school days.
  • Downtime keeps Roblox closed at night.

3. Lock Roblox when it matters

A daily limit doesn't help at dinner or when homework comes first. With Blok you add Roblox to the apps you lock, and tap LOCK on your own iPhone: until you unlock, for 30 minutes, 1 or 2 hours, or on schedules. When it's unlocked, Roblox's own limits still apply.

Questions

Can I limit how long my child plays Roblox?

Yes. With a linked parent account, Roblox lets you set a daily time limit for children under 13. On the iPhone you can also give Roblox an App Limit in Screen Time, or lock it with Blok.

How do I stop my child buying Robux on the iPhone?

Turn on Ask to Buy in Family Sharing so every purchase needs your approval, and set a spending limit in Roblox's parental controls.

Do Roblox parental controls cover chat outside Roblox?

No. They only cover Roblox itself. Children often move conversations to other apps, so talk with your child about who they play and chat with.
