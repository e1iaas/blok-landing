How to block TikTok, YouTube and games on your child's iPhone

Updated 4 November 2026

To block specific apps like TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat or Roblox on a child's iPhone, either give them a very short daily limit with Apple's Screen Time, or choose those apps in a parental control app like Blok and lock them from your own phone whenever you want. In both cases, also block Safari and app installs, or the websites still work.

With Apple Screen Time

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings → Screen Time and tap your child's name under Family.
  2. Tap App Limits → Add Limit, choose the apps (for example TikTok and YouTube) or a whole category like Games, and set the daily time.
  3. Turn on Block at End of Limit, so your child needs your approval for more time.
  4. Use Downtime to block everything except allowed apps at night or during school hours.

App Limits count time per day; they don't give you a switch to block an app right now and open it again an hour later.

With Blok: choose the apps, lock them when you decide

  1. Install Blok on both iPhones and pair them with a code.
  2. On your child's phone, choose the apps to lock, for example TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat, Instagram, Roblox and other games. The choice stays on your child's phone.
  3. Tap LOCK on your own phone: until you unlock, for 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours, or on a schedule for homework and bedtime.

Everything else keeps working, including Phone and Messages. Up to three children's phones can be locked, each on its own.

Close the gaps

  • The browser. TikTok and YouTube also work in Safari. Lock Safari too, or limit websites under Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions.
  • New apps. Under Content & Privacy Restrictions → App Store Purchases, set Installing Apps to Don't Allow, so a blocked app can't be swapped for a similar one.
  • The passcode. Use a Screen Time passcode or Blok's parent PIN that your child doesn't know.

Questions

Does blocking an app delete my child's data in it?

No. A Screen Time lock only stops the app from opening. Accounts, messages and game progress are still there when the app is unlocked.

Can I block TikTok only at bedtime?

Yes. Use Downtime in Screen Time, or a schedule in Blok that locks the chosen apps every evening and unlocks them in the morning.

Can my child get around the block?

Not by closing the app or restarting the phone, because iOS enforces the lock. Keep your passcode or PIN secret and block new app installs. Only a factory reset removes the lock, and no parental control can prevent that.
