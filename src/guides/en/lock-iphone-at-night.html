How to lock your child's iPhone at night

Updated 8 November 2026

To lock your child's iPhone at night, set a Downtime schedule in Screen Time from your own iPhone, for example from 20:30 to 07:00, and keep Phone and Messages under Always Allowed. A parental control app like Blok can do the same with a bedtime schedule, plus a one-tap lock for nights that run late. Charging the phone outside the bedroom helps most of all.

With Screen Time: a Downtime schedule

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings → Screen Time and tap your child's name.
  2. Tap Downtime, turn on Scheduled and set the times. You can choose different times for school nights and weekends.
  3. Turn on Block at Downtime, so your child can't just tap "ignore".
  4. Under Always Allowed, keep Phone and Messages so your child can reach you, and remove games and social media.

With Blok: a bedtime schedule and a lock button

In Blok you add a schedule for bedtime that locks the apps you picked and unlocks them in the morning. On a night when the schedule doesn't fit, like a sleepover or an exam tomorrow, tap LOCK on your phone or lock for an hour. Your phone shows when your child's phone has confirmed the lock.

Make bedtime easier

  • Charge phones outside the bedroom. Sweden's Public Health Agency recommends keeping phones out of the bedroom at night. A charging spot in the kitchen works for the whole family.
  • Screens off a while before bed. Many families switch off 30 to 60 minutes before lights out.
  • Get an alarm clock. "I need it as an alarm" is the most common reason a phone stays in the bedroom.
  • Agree on the time together. A bedtime your child helped choose gets less pushback.

Questions

Can my child still call me at night if the iPhone is locked?

Yes, if Phone is in Always Allowed in Screen Time, or not among the apps you lock in Blok. Emergency calls always work.

What time should a child's phone be turned off at night?

There's no single right time. Many families lock the phone 30 to 60 minutes before bedtime and unlock it in the morning. Pick a time that gives your child enough sleep for their age.

Can I lock my child's iPhone at night without them knowing?

Your child will see that apps are locked. It works best when you've agreed on the bedtime lock together, so it's a family rule and not a surprise.
